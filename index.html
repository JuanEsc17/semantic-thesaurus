Semantic Thesaurus Construction & Pivot Determination

Based on Moon, Lee & Chi (2022) - "Automated system for construction specification review using NLP" (Advanced Engineering Informatics).

1. The Recursive Loop Problem

In natural language domain corpora, synonym detection algorithms easily fall into recursive loops: word $A$ is similar to $B$, $B$ is similar to $C$, and $C$ points back to $A$ (e.g. Doctor ↔ Physician ↔ Medical Specialist). Which term should be standardized as the canonical descriptor?

2. The PivotTermDetermination Algorithm (PageRank-Inspired)

The authors formulated the concepts of Inflow and Outflow based on directional similarities:

(1) Rank Weight: g(w', W) = ( n(W) - index(w'|sorted(W)) ) / n(W)
(2) Inflow: inflow_w = ∑ g(w', W_out(w')) × c(w, w')
(3) Outflow: outflow_w = ∑ g(w', W_out(w)) × c(w, w')
(4) Flow Margin: f_w = inflow_w - outflow_w

3. Decision Rules

  • If $f_w > 0$: The term has positive incoming authority → Keep as Pivot Term (Preferred Descriptor).
  • If $f_w \le 0$: Replace with the most similar word whose flow margin is positive: Target → USE → Pivot.
